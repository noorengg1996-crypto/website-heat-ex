## 🏭 Corporate Overview

**Northern HeatEx Corporation** is a premier manufacturer and refurbishment specialist for heavy industrial thermal exchange equipment and high-precision white metal rotating bearings. Headquartered with manufacturing works in Haridwar, Uttarakhand, Northern HeatEx Corporation caters to the demanding requirements of nuclear, hydro, thermal power utilities, defense installations, and chemical processing complexes.

### Core Capabilities:
- **Heat Exchanger Fabrication:** ASME & TEMA compliant Shell & Tube heat exchangers, Stator Air Coolers, Hydrogen Coolers, and Oil Coolers.
- **Turnkey 10-Step Retubing:** In-situ and shopfloor re-tubing, tube pulling, torque-controlled pneumatic expansion, hydrotesting up to 100 bar, and eddy current testing.
- **White Metal Bearing Re-Babbitting:** ASTM B23 Grade 2 centrifugally cast journal, thrust, and guide bearings finished to ±0.01 mm CNC tolerance.
- **Quality Certifications:** ISO 9001:2015 accredited facility with 100% material traceability and non-destructive examination (NDE).
